30 Years of Reign

Mehmed II was born on 30 March 1432 in Edirne. The son of Sultan Murad II, he demonstrated extraordinary intelligence and military genius from an early age.

First ascending to the throne at just 12 years old at his father's request, the young prince never lost his passion for knowledge. He mastered eight languages — Arabic, Persian, Latin, Italian, Slavic, Greek, and more. He gained deep expertise in mathematics, geography, astronomy, and philosophy.

A lover of poetry, he wrote Ottoman verse under the pen name "Avni". Mehmed was not merely a warrior — he was an intellectual who championed art and science, inviting scholars and artists from across the globe to his court.

When his father died in 1451, nineteen-year-old Mehmed ascended the throne for the second time, ready to execute the grand plan that would alter the course of history.

Legacy

What He Left Behind

✦
📜

Law Code

He compiled the "Kanunname-i Al-i Osman," codifying Ottoman law and bringing order to state governance.

🕌

Fatih Complex

He commissioned a grand külliye in Istanbul — a complex of mosque, madrasas, soup kitchen, and caravanserai that became a center of learning.

🎨

Patron of the Arts

He invited Venetian painter Gentile Bellini to Istanbul — making him the first Ottoman sultan to have his portrait painted.

📚

Center of Knowledge

By building eight madrasas, he transformed Istanbul into the intellectual capital of the Islamic world, welcoming scholars from across the globe.

🏙️

Rebirth of Istanbul

After the conquest, he rebuilt the ruined city from the ground up, settling people of diverse nations and creating a cosmopolitan capital.

⚖️

Policy of Tolerance

He protected the Greek Orthodox Patriarchate and allowed various religious communities to live under their own laws.
